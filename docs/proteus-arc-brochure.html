Proteus Arc
01 / Fundamental vision

A public research mandate

Make the invisible course of Alzheimer’s inspectable.

Proteus Arc asks whether changing relationships across EEG channels can help researchers study Alzheimer’s as a dynamic system—while keeping source, time, uncertainty, and missing evidence attached.

SourceEEG over time

Preserve channel identity, event timing, quality flags, and visible gaps.

RelationChanging dependencies

Explore whether relationships carry information that isolated averages lose.

TopologyGroups beyond pairs

Represent proposed higher-order structure without hiding it inside a score.

ContextAI questionnaire

Add governed lived context while preserving self-report limits and human review.

BoundaryUncertainty in frame

Show provenance, absent inputs, model limits, and what remains unvalidated.

Fundamental commitment: a brain-health hypothesis should be legible before it becomes influential.

02 / Access + knowledge

Widen the research door

Specialist scarcity should not decide who gets seen.

01

More accessible research inputs

EEG is comparatively portable, repeatable, and time-sensitive. A clinician-governed questionnaire could add structured context between visits. Neither replaces biomarkers, imaging, neurological examination, or clinical judgment.

02

Knowledge as infrastructure

Methods, limits, and receipts should be understandable to more scientists, clinicians, families, and builders. Shared questions can strengthen studies and inspire future cures.

03

Evidence people can challenge

Every interpretation should retain its inputs, proposed operation, uncertainty, and missing evidence. “I don’t know” belongs in the interface.

Boundary: This vision does not promise a cure, diagnosis, screening service, research enrollment, or clinical access today.

04 / Boundaries + correspondence

Trust begins with what is withheld

Nothing becomes trustworthy by disappearing.

What is live
A deterministic public prospectus, inspectable interface simulation, and downloadable evidence record.
What is not live
EEG ingestion, questionnaire delivery, artifact processing, model training, inference, calibration, validation, diagnosis, or care.
Human role
Question assumptions, inspect provenance, review evidence, and decide whether a research claim deserves further study.
Privacy
Do not send personal medical data or EEG files by email. The contact inbox is correspondence—not clinical intake.

Make the method more rigorous.

Research collaboration, scientific review, access, public knowledge, product, and responsible AI.
