## Category defaults to reject

- Left-copy/right-abstract-visual hero
- Cyan or violet “science” accent on black
- Brain silhouette made from generic nodes and edges
- Pill CTAs or clipped technical button frames
- Fixed glass navigation
- Numbered marketing sections
- Alternating copy/illustration rows
- Equal-weight capability or audience cards
- Decorative console output and circular progress rings
- Multiple autonomous loops competing for attention
- “Learn more” without a named destination
- Claim-first, limitation-later copy

## Chosen concept — Evidence Prospectus

“The IPO for Alzheimer’s” becomes structural rather than rhetorical. It is explicitly **not a stock offering**. It is a public offering of method: how a research hypothesis moves from EEG traces to temporal events, pairwise relationships, higher-order topology, uncertainty, and provenance.

### Surface commitments

- **Public route:** Decide / Learn. One idea becomes inspectable at a time.
- **Workspace:** Command / Inspect. One evidence specimen is manipulated and examined; there is no marketing hero or dashboard sidebar.

### Continuous scientific object

A single deterministic evidence field spans the public journey and workspace:

1. Raw stacked EEG traces
2. Marked temporal events
3. Pairwise relationships
4. Higher-order loops linking groups, not only pairs
5. Uncertainty bands and provenance receipts

The object is deliberately not a brain silhouette. It makes the method visible through transformation rather than illustrating the word “brain.”

### Evidence lens

Pointer or touch focus acts as an evidence lens. It reveals local source channels, transformation stage, retained context, and uncertainty. It does not repel particles or add decorative glow. Reduced motion presents a static composite; hidden/offscreen states stop scheduling frames.

### Public and researcher depth

The two audiences bifurcate inside the same composition:

- **Read the thesis:** plain-language annotations and purpose
- **Inspect the method:** channel labels, transformation receipts, uncertainty, provenance, and explicit simulation boundaries

No audience cards are used.

### Typography

- **Newsreader Variable:** human thesis, consequence, and major editorial clauses
- **Recursive Variable:** navigation, body, controls, and method; its sans-to-mono axis expresses the transition from public explanation to technical inspection

Type change carries hierarchy before boxes, icons, or color.

### Color

- Continuous black field
- Warm paper-white text
- A single signal-blue accent, applied after user review; orange is reserved for the native scrollbar
- Uncertainty uses opacity, line density, and hatching—not a rainbow palette

### Action grammar

An **open-clause action** is unboxed. Its baseline opens around a moving locus and reveals the next state. The visible design is a line of type and a rule; the semantic target remains at least 44px. No pill, rounded rectangle, cut corner, terminal ornament, or icon topper.

### Conversion and trust

- Hero explains the IPO metaphor immediately.
- Primary action: **Read the prospectus**
- Secondary action: **Inspect the instrument simulation**
- Claim and limitation appear together.
- Research evidence, provenance, and product boundaries are part of the journey—not footer legal copy.
- No fake metrics, publications, approvals, partners, testimonials, patient stories, outcomes, or model scores.

## Risks and controls

- **Financial metaphor risk:** disambiguate in the first viewport.
- **Prospectus density risk:** use sparse margin annotations and progressive depth.
- **Visualization accessibility risk:** provide semantic text for every state; never require pointer interaction.
- **False-capability risk:** use “proposed,” “would,” “fixture,” and “interface simulation” beside every operational state.
- **Scroll-performance risk:** native scrolling only; animation reads scroll progress without changing wheel physics.
- **Mobile risk:** replace persistent split composition with one trace field and stacked clauses; touch toggles the evidence lens.

## Decision

Proceed with Evidence Prospectus, incorporating the Living Signal Ledger’s transformation receipt and the Cortical Instrument’s shared coordinate system. The existing Iteration 03 visual primitives are removed rather than restyled.
